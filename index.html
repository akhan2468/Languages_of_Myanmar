Languages of Myanmar

106 languages from 5 language families. Where are they spoken, are they written, and which ones are disappearing? Click a pin on the map to see one language.

Centre = Myanmar, then language family, then branch (biggest branches labelled). Each dot at the edge is one language, coloured by endangerment. Hover a dot to see its name.

Data: Glottolog 5 (glottolog.org) for language locations, families and endangerment levels. State borders: Natural Earth. Scripts: my own research on Wikipedia for 23 major languages. Glottolog gives one point per language, so a pin marks the centre of where it is spoken.
